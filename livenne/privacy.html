Livenne Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 16, 2026

Livenne ("Livenne", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Livenne mobile application.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

Livenne is designed primarily as a local, on-device application. You do not need to create an account to use the app.

Information such as the following may be stored locally on your device:

  • Room photos you choose or capture
  • Saved room designs and furniture placements
  • Furniture and decor images you import
  • Custom item details such as store, price, product link, and notes
  • Application preferences and appearance settings

Livenne does not operate its own cloud account system or server for uploading and storing your room photos or saved designs.

Locally stored information generally remains on your device until you delete it through the app, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

2. Photos and Camera Access

Livenne may request permission to access your camera or photo library so that you can select, capture, import, edit, or save images.

These permissions are used only when required for features you choose to use.

3. Background Removal and Image Processing

Certain image-processing features, such as background removal, may use Google ML Kit technology.

Subject segmentation used for these features is processed on the device. Livenne does not intentionally upload your room or furniture images to Livenne-operated servers for this processing.

4. Advertising

Livenne uses Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process certain information for purposes such as advertising, analytics, measurement, and fraud prevention. Depending on your device, region, consent choices, and advertising settings, this may include:

  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad interactions
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Advertising-related information

Information collected by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies and terms.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at: Google – How information from sites or apps that use our services is used .

Google's Privacy Policy is available at: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

5. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, Livenne uses Google's consent management tools to request and manage advertising-related consent.

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other applicable regions may be presented with privacy choices regarding personalized advertising and the processing of personal data.

You may be able to review or change your advertising privacy choices through the Privacy Options feature available in Livenne.

6. iOS App Tracking Transparency

On supported Apple devices, Livenne may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before allowing tracking that requires such permission.

If you decline tracking permission, the app can still display advertisements, but advertising identifiers used for tracking will not be available in the same manner.

7. Sharing of Information

Livenne itself does not operate a service for selling your room photos, saved designs, custom furniture images, or product notes.

Third-party services integrated into the app, including Google AdMob, may process or share information as described in their own privacy policies and as permitted by your consent choices and applicable law.

8. Saved Images and Sharing

If you choose to save an exported design to your photo library or share an image through another application, that action is performed at your request.

Information shared with another application or service is subject to that application's or service's privacy practices.

9. Children's Privacy

Livenne is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly design the service for the collection of personal information from children under 13.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to minimize unnecessary collection and transmission of user information. However, no device, application, transmission method, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to personal data under applicable privacy laws.

Because most Livenne design data is stored locally on your device, you can remove locally stored projects and imported items using the application's deletion features or by removing the application's local data.

Advertising-related privacy choices may also be managed using the privacy controls provided within the app or through your device's privacy and advertising settings.

12. Third-Party Services

Livenne may use third-party technologies and services, including:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Google ML Kit
  • Apple and Android operating-system services

These third parties may maintain their own privacy policies governing their processing of information.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Livenne evolves, when third-party services change, or when legal or platform requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was most recently updated.
